# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this community Service, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

> This document is a practical template for a community forum. It is not legal advice. Adapt it to your hosting setup, plugins, analytics tools, and local privacy laws (including GDPR/CCPA where applicable), and have it reviewed before publishing.

## 1. Who is responsible

The operators of this community are responsible for personal data processed through the Service. Contact details are listed on the About page.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account and profile data

- Username, email address, password (stored as a secure hash)
- Optional profile fields you choose to provide (name, bio, avatar, links, locale, timezone)
- Role, trust level, and group memberships

### Content and activity data

- Posts, messages, uploads, likes, bookmarks, and similar interactions
- Drafts and notification preferences
- Logs needed for security and reliability (IP address, user agent, timestamps, error events)

### Technical and cookie data

- Session cookies and similar technologies required for login and preferences
- Optional analytics or anti-abuse signals, if enabled by the site

We do not require you to provide more personal information than needed to operate the forum.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, maintain, and secure the Service
- Authenticate users and send transactional email (verification, password reset, notifications you enable)
- Moderate content and enforce Community Guidelines / Terms of Service
- Diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve performance
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Legal bases (where required)

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, we typically rely on:

- **Contract / service delivery** — creating an account and operating the forum
- **Legitimate interests** — security, abuse prevention, product improvement
- **Consent** — where required for optional cookies, marketing, or non-essential processing
- **Legal obligation** — when we must retain or disclose data by law

## 5. Sharing and processors

We may share data with:

- Hosting, email delivery, CDN, backup, and security providers that process data on our instructions
- Moderators and administrators acting for the community
- Authorities when legally required

Public posts are visible according to category and site permissions. Private messages are visible to participants and, when necessary, to staff for abuse handling.

## 6. International transfers

If you access the Service from another country, your information may be processed in the region where the Service is hosted or where our processors operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 7. Retention

- Account and profile data are kept while your account remains active
- Public posts may remain as part of the community archive unless removed under policy or law
- Security logs are retained for a limited period needed for safety and troubleshooting
- Backups are rotated on a schedule and then deleted

You may request account deletion subject to legal retention needs and residual backup cycles.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict certain personal data, and to object to some processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.

To exercise these rights, contact us via the About page. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority where applicable.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under the digital consent age in your region (for example, under 13 in some jurisdictions, or under 16 where required). Do not register if you are below the applicable age.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect accounts and data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong unique password and enable available account protections.

## 11. Third-party services

Plugins, login providers, analytics, or embeds may process data under their own policies. Review those policies before use.

## 12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact information on the About page.
