# Community Guidelines

Welcome. These guidelines help keep discussions useful, respectful, and safe for everyone.

## 1. Be respectful

- Assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, or doxxing.
- No discriminatory language based on identity, background, or beliefs.

## 2. Stay on topic

- Post in the most relevant category.
- Prefer clear titles and a short summary of the problem or goal.
- Search before posting. Link related threads when helpful.

## 3. Share useful technical content

- Include enough context: software/version, model setup, expected vs actual result, and what you already tried.
- Prefer evidence over speculation: screenshots, logs, simplified examples, and references.
- Do not present others’ work as your own. Cite sources.

## 4. Keep the community healthy

- No spam, excessive self-promotion, or affiliate dumping.
- One account per person. Do not evade suspensions or bans.
- Do not post malware, phishing links, or intentionally harmful files.

## 5. Privacy and safety

- Do not share private personal data (yours or others’) without clear permission.
- Be careful with proprietary models, client data, and confidential project files.
- Report suspicious or abusive content to staff instead of escalating publicly.

## 6. Moderation

Moderators may edit, move, close, or remove content that breaks these guidelines. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent restrictions.

Questions about these guidelines? Contact the moderation team through the site contact options on the About page.
